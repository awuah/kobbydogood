About The Initiative

A Vision of Personal Grassroots Service for Takoradi

KOBBY DO GOOD was founded as the personal social development initiative of Kwabena Okyere Darko-Mensah (popularly known as "Kobby"). Built as his own private endeavor and financed entirely from his personal resources without any government or public funds, this initiative is centered on building human capital, nurturing youth innovation, and providing direct safety nets for vulnerable households across Takoradi.

Driven by personal conviction and dedicated private philanthropy, Kwabena Darko-Mensah established four autonomous, high-impact pillars engineered to bring immediate transformation to the streets, markets, and communities of Takoradi.

Compassionate Giving

Direct support for healthcare emergencies, school fees, and widow empowerment.

Digital & Tech Skills

Preparing Takoradi's youth for the digital economy through modern labs.

Rapid Employability

Free certified driving school providing licensed pathways to jobs.

Youth Investment & Prosperity

Real enterprise investments (not machine handouts) focused on value addition to create lasting wealth.

Youth Enterprise & Value Addition

KOBBY YOUTH INVESTMENT INITIATIVE (KYII)

Core Investment Principle: We are not sharing items or distributing machines. KYII makes real, strategic capital investments in our youth designed to create sustainable prosperity and spur further investment in other youth and the wider community.

KYII prioritizes individuals and youth groups who are ready to put investment to work immediately to create measurable prosperity. We are particularly interested in processing, manufacturing, and value-addition projects that turn raw potential into finished economic value.

Key Investment & Accountability Guidelines:

Investment for Prosperity: Real investment in youth enterprise — not machine or item handouts — to generate compounding community wealth.
Immediate Work Readiness: Priority is always given to people ready to deploy capital immediately into productive work.
Value Addition & Processing: Special focus on projects and people engaged in processing and tangible value addition.
Mandatory Video & Product Proof: Beneficiaries must submit video evidence of active operations, tools at work, and finished products.
Performance-Based Follow-on Funding: New and additional investments into groups are strictly decided based on the video evidence, product output, and performance reports received.

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about how the KOBBY DO GOOD programs function and how you or your ward can participate.

Any youth resident within the Takoradi area who is at least 18 years of age, holds a valid Ghana Card, and is committed to attending the mandatory theoretical and practical sessions can register via freedrive.kobbydogood.org or at the foundation project desk.
TYC is open from Monday to Saturday to students, freelancers, and young creators. The digital lab, reading spaces, and studios are completely free. You can view schedules, register for workshops, and book studio slots directly at TYC.kobbydogood.org.
Residents facing health emergencies, educational dues distress, or vulnerable circumstances can submit their documentation directly via the portal at Giving.kobbydogood.org or visit the project desk in Takoradi.
KYII is strictly an investment in youth enterprise — not a machine-sharing or handout scheme. We make strategic capital investments in youth who are ready to put resources to work immediately to generate prosperity, with strong preference for processing and value-addition ventures. Future and additional investment into any group depends entirely on the video evidence of their work, products generated, and progress reports received.
